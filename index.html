Prototype

Museum of Seeing

A children's aesthetic adventure app prototype for ages 7-9. This mockup tests one complete loop: explore a world, notice a work, make something, and collect the result.

Flow

  1. Choose a world from the map
  2. Play the Daily Spark mission
  3. Create with colors and shapes
  4. Unlock a new collection card

What To Look For

  • Does it feel playful instead of school-like?
  • Does the mission naturally lead into creation?
  • Does the reward feel like identity, not points?
